## Sending capacity — verified numbers (2026-10)

Researched, not assumed. Free Gmail = **500 recipients/day** documented cap; Workspace =
2,000 msgs / 10,000 recipients. **Cold-outreach safe figure is 20–50/day per inbox** —
above that it's spam-foldered. My earlier "8–12/day" was the cautious cold-start figure,
not the ceiling — corrected in the README.

Ranked: browser automation (worst, lock risk) < SMTP+app password (20–50/day, low risk) <
domain mailbox + Postmark/Resend/SES (scales, best deliverability).

**Key reframe given to user:** send capacity is *not* the bottleneck — targets are. 36
qualified, 9 verified emails. 500/day needs 500 vetted targets. Grow the list first.
