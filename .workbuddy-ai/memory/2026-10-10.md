# 2026-10-10

## Backlink automation — architecture agreed, plan delivered

User confirmed they can give Playwright browser access to seolaunchers@gmail.com and asked for the automation route. Confirmed direction: **Tier A editorial only**, **top 20-30 pages** (chose top 25).

**Answer given on self-publish platforms (they asked directly):** ~zero for authority/ranking. Verified empirically — a live dev.to article carried **16 `rel="nofollow"`** on external links; Google's "Qualify outbound links" doc says UGC links should be `ugc`/`nofollow` and *"will generally not be followed"*. Value split: ~90%+ of any ranking effect is Tier A; self-publish <5%. Recommended 3-5 posts max, brand only.

**Built:**
- `Tier-A-Submission-Pack.xlsx` — 7 sheets: How to use, Wave 1 (25 pages), Tier A Targets (80, verified), Pitch Templates, Author Bio Block, Tracker.
- `backlink-automation-plan.md` — 6-stage pipeline, working code for each stage.

**Key QC finding:** verified all 80 Tier A submission URLs live. **25 were dead or bot-blocked** (15-17 × 404, 8 × 403, 2 unreachable). 404s include Business2Community, Matador Network, National Law Review, JURIST, LiveLaw, Go Overseas, EDUopinions, GoAbroad, Travel Awaits, Hidden Compass, Opinio Juris, Just Security, Study International, InfoQ, The Mover. The source spreadsheet was compiled 2026-10-09 and already has ~19% stale URLs — **always re-verify before spending time on a target**.

**Technical positions recorded:**
- Gmail via Playwright persistent context (`launch_persistent_context`, `channel="chrome"`, `headless=False`) works but is fragile — Gmail DOM is obfuscated and Google challenges automation. **IMAP + app password is materially better** and is the recommendation.
- CAPTCHA cannot be automated. Design for human-in-the-loop: fill the form, `page.pause()` at the CAPTCHA, human solves, resume. Expect 30-50% of form targets to hit CAPTCHA/Cloudflare.
- Gmail send cap ~8-12 pitches/day with 90-240s randomised gaps, else spam foldering. Better: domain mailbox (outreach@commonwealthmigration.ca) with SPF/DKIM/DMARC.

**Still blocked on user:** mailbox access method, author identity (name/title/CICC number/bio/headshot), explicit risk sign-off, send-from decision.

**Realistic expectation set:** 10-25 published links from 80 Tier A routes in 3-6 months. Bottleneck is editorial acceptance, not submission volume.
