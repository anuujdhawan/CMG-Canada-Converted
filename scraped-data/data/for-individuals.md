## Page Content

## Choose Your Pathway

From Express Entry to work permits, study, and family sponsorship - we support every major route to Canada.

### Express Entry

Federal skilled worker, Canadian Experience Class, PNP-linked. CRS optimization and category-based draws.

### Work Permits

LMIA, LMIA-exempt, open work permits, PGWP, spousal, IEC. Find the right permit for your situation.

### Study Permits

Study in Canada and explore study-to-PR pathways. PGWP and provincial options.

### Family Sponsorship

Spousal, common-law, parent and grandparent (PGP), super visa. Reunite your family.

### Visitor Visa & eTA

Visit Canada for tourism, business, or to visit family. eTA for visa-exempt nationals.

### Citizenship & PR Card

Canadian citizenship applications and PR card renewal. Eligibility and process guidance.

## Find Out Where You Stand
