Executive Summary
During September 2026, Loud Launchers completed a substantial SEO, GEO and AEO
improvement programme for cwmigrationgroup.ae. The work went beyond basic metadata
changes: the website received a major content expansion, stronger answer-focused content,
improved crawl surfaces, better internal linking, structured data, local entity signals and new
trust-building features.
The site previously had important content areas with limited depth and incomplete AEO and GEO
optimisation. These gaps were addressed by adding significantly more useful content to priority
pages and creating a larger, clearer information structure for search engines, AI systems and users.
Payment gateway work is excluded from this report because it was outside the SEO, GEO and
AEO completion scope.
