Current Website Shortcomings - Summary
The website already has a substantial content structure, including detailed visa pages, service pages,
blogs, tools, calculators and trust-related information. However, some areas require further improvement
to strengthen its SEO, GEO and AEO performance. These include technical SEO verification, page-level
keyword alignment, content consistency, internal linking, structured data, local Dubai/GCC signals,
question-based answers and authority-building backlinks. Some existing content also requires review for
outdated information, repetitive wording, incomplete answers and inconsistent presentation across
different pages.
The September action plan will focus on improving these existing areas while adding fresh, relevant
content and strengthening the website’s visibility for Australian immigration searches from Dubai, the UAE,
GCC countries and the wider Middle East.
The following activities will be completed for this website during September 2026:
1. Keyword and competitor research for Australian immigration services targeting Dubai, UAE,
GCC and the wider Middle East.
2. Identification of relevant low-, moderate- and high-competition keywords for skilled
migration, employer-sponsored visas, student visas, family visas and visitor visas.
3. Creation and publication of 10–12 SEO-optimized blog articles based on relevant Australian
immigration search topics.
4. Optimization of existing priority pages, including the homepage, services page, About page
and important visa pathway pages.
5. Optimization of page titles, meta descriptions, headings, content structure, image alt text
and internal links.
6. Improvement of existing website content for search intent, readability, accuracy and user
engagement.
7. Expansion of FAQ sections across existing website pages by adding relevant,
search-focused questions and clear answers to improve user understanding, SEO visibility and
AEO performance.
8. Addition and improvement of question-based content, FAQs and direct answers for AEO
purposes.
9. Implementation or improvement of relevant structured data and schema markup for
services, organizations, articles, breadcrumbs and FAQs where applicable.
10. Technical SEO review covering sitemap, robots.txt, canonical URLs, indexing, redirects,
broken links, page errors and basic mobile performance.
