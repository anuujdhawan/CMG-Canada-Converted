## Quick answer

There is no single standard price for an immigration consultant in Ontario. A fee depends on the application, complexity, family members, document work, and whether representation continues after filing. Ask for a written, itemized agreement that separates professional fees from government charges and confirms what happens if extra work is needed.

There is no single price that applies to every immigration-consulting service in Ontario. The total depends on the application type, the amount of document review and drafting involved, urgency, family members, prior refusals, and whether the work includes representation after submission. Government application fees are separate from a consultant’s professional fee.

IRCC recommends that clients discuss the services and fee before hiring a paid representative, and get a written contract that clearly lists the work and cost. Before signing, use the government’s [representative-selection guidance](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigration-citizenship-representative/choose.html) and verify that a paid consultant is authorized.

## Questions to ask before you hire

- What exact tasks are included, and what work is outside the quoted scope?
- Are government fees, translations, medical exams, and courier costs separate?
- Who will prepare the application and who will review it?
- How will additional document requests, changes, or urgent deadlines be handled?
- What is the cancellation or refund policy?
- Will you receive a signed written agreement and copies of the filings?

For a Brampton consultation, ask for an itemized explanation of the service, not only a headline price. Compare equivalent scopes; a low quote may exclude important work, while a higher fee is not a guarantee of approval.

Do not pay anyone who promises guaranteed PR, a guaranteed visa, or a special way to influence an officer. IRCC decides applications. A consultant can explain and perform contracted work, but cannot guarantee the decision.

## Compare quotes by scope, not by the headline number

Ask each provider to quote the same task. For example, clarify whether the service includes eligibility review, form preparation, document organization, submission, responding to an additional-document request, and communication with IRCC. A quote that covers only form entry cannot be compared directly with a full representation package.

Request an agreement that names the representative, describes the services, sets out the fee and payment schedule, lists disbursements, and explains cancellation, refunds, and work outside scope. Ask whether translation, courier, medical exam, biometrics, police certificates, and government application fees are separate. IRCC fees and a consultant’s professional fee are different costs; verify government charges on the official fee page at the time you apply.

Before paying a retainer, confirm the person is authorized to provide paid immigration advice. Use IRCC’s representative-selection guidance and the relevant regulator’s public register. Ask who will actually prepare the file and who will communicate with you; the person who sells the service may not be the person doing the work.

Be cautious about guarantees, pressure to pay immediately, or a price that depends on an officer’s decision. A representative can provide contracted services but cannot promise a visa, nomination, or permanent residence. Keep the signed agreement, invoices, receipts, and copies of every filed document.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does an immigration consultant cost in Ontario?

Fees vary by the service and file complexity. Ask for an itemized written quote for the exact scope; there is no single fee that applies to every immigration matter.

### Are government fees included in a consultant’s fee?

Not necessarily. Ask the provider to list professional fees, IRCC fees, translations, medical exams, courier costs, and other disbursements separately in the agreement.

### How can I check whether a paid consultant is authorized?

Use IRCC’s official representative-selection guidance and verify the consultant in the College of Immigration and Citizenship Consultants public register before signing or paying.
