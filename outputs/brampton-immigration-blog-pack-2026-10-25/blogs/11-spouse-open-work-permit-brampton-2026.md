## Quick answer

A spouse or common-law partner may qualify for an open work permit only if the principal applicant and the relationship meet a current IRCC pathway. The rules differ for spouses of students, foreign workers, and some PR applicants. A marriage or Brampton address alone is not enough; check the principal applicant’s exact program, occupation, permit validity, and required documents.

A spouse or common-law partner does not qualify for an open work permit simply because the other person studies or works in Canada. Since 2025, eligibility has been limited and depends on the principal applicant’s study program, occupation, permit validity, and sometimes an eligible pathway to permanent residence. IRCC has added specific 2026 exceptions, so older articles may no longer reflect the current rules.

For some international students, the spouse may qualify where the student is in a doctoral program, a master’s program of at least 16 months, or a listed professional or eligible program. For some foreign workers, the spouse may qualify if the worker is employed in TEER 0 or 1, or selected TEER 2 or 3 occupations, and the permit has the required validity. Other pathways have separate rules. Review the official [student spouse guidance](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/study-canada/work/help-your-spouse-common-law-partner-work-canada.html) and [worker family eligibility page](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/work-canada/special-instructions/spouses-dependent-children/eligibility.html).

## Before applying from Brampton

Identify the principal applicant’s exact status and program or occupation. Check permit expiry dates, the spouse’s current status in Canada, proof of the relationship, and any application deadlines. Use IRCC’s eligibility questions and document list rather than relying on a generic job-title description.

A Brampton address does not change federal eligibility. If a family member is not eligible for an open work permit, there may be another route to visit, study, or apply for a work permit, but each option has its own criteria.

Avoid paying someone who promises that an open work permit is automatic. Ask for the exact rule they believe applies and compare it with the current IRCC page.

Check the principal applicant’s permit expiry date and the spouse’s current status before preparing forms. The spouse may need to apply together with the principal applicant or separately, depending on the pathway. ## Frequently asked questions

### Does a spouse of every international student qualify for an open work permit?

No. Eligibility is limited to specific study programs and other conditions set by IRCC. Check the student’s exact credential and program against the current list.

### Can a spouse apply because their partner has a Canadian work permit?

Not automatically. The worker’s occupation, permit validity, and other pathway conditions matter. Compare the actual work duties with IRCC’s current eligibility rules.

### Does a Brampton address affect spouse open work permit eligibility?

No. The rules are federal. The relevant facts are the principal applicant’s qualifying status or program, the relationship, and the application requirements.
