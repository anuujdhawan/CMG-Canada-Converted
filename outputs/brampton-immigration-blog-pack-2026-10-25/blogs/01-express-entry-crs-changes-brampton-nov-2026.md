## Quick answer

Starting 3 November 2026, IRCC is changing the Express Entry CRS treatment of certain Canadian work experience and qualifying job offers. The new Labour Market Integration factor awards the higher of the two applicable options rather than adding both. Brampton residence does not change the federal calculation; accurate profile details and supporting employment records still matter.

## What changes for Express Entry candidates on 3 November?

IRCC has announced that it will change how Express Entry awards points for Canadian work experience and qualifying job offers on 3 November 2026. A new Labour Market Integration factor will give points for either eligible Canadian work experience or a qualifying job offer in an eligible high-wage occupation. IRCC says a candidate will receive whichever of those two options gives the higher score, rather than combining both. Existing profiles are expected to be recalculated, and invitation rounds may pause for up to two weeks while that happens. Check the [official IRCC change notice](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/express-entry/changes.html) before relying on a summary.

For people in Brampton and Peel, the practical job is to make sure the profile is accurate and the supporting records are ready. The city where you live does not change federal eligibility, but a well-organized employment file can make it easier to verify dates, duties, hours, and the employer’s details.

## A short preparation checklist

1. Save your current Express Entry profile details and CRS calculation for comparison.
2. Match each work-history entry to employer letters, pay records, tax documents, and accurate dates.
3. Check that your NOC code reflects the duties you actually performed, not only your job title.
4. If you have a job offer, confirm that its terms and employer information are genuine and documented.
5. Recheck your score after IRCC updates its system. Do not assume an old calculator or screenshot reflects the new factor.

The November change does not make every job offer eligible for new CRS points, and the job-offer scoring rule is distinct from some program-eligibility requirements. An LMIA will no longer be required for a qualifying Express Entry job offer under the new rules, but IRCC will still assess whether the offer is genuine. Read the official instructions carefully, especially if the online profile fields have not yet been updated.

A lower or higher score is not an invitation by itself. Draw type, eligibility, pool ranking, and the profile information on record still matter. Use “CRS score help Brampton” as a starting point for research, not a promise of a particular result.


## How to check your profile before and after the change

Use a before-and-after review rather than trying to predict a draw cutoff. First, save your current profile answers and score, plus the date you last updated the profile. Next, review every Canadian and foreign work entry against the employer letter and payroll records. An employment letter should describe duties, dates, hours, wage, and the employer; a title alone may not explain whether the work fits a claimed NOC.

For a job offer, keep the signed offer and information about the employer, role, location, wage, and employment terms. Then compare those facts with IRCC’s new definition and any occupation or wage conditions in its current notice. Do not assume that a job offer used for an LMIA, a work permit, or an Ontario nomination automatically qualifies for this CRS factor. These are related but separate rules.

After the announced change takes effect, sign in and confirm what the system displays. IRCC has said existing profiles are expected to be recalculated and that rounds may pause for up to two weeks during the changeover. If the online profile has not refreshed or a field is unclear, follow current IRCC instructions and retain a record of any correction. A score is only one part of selection: eligibility for a managed program, the type of round, and the pool ranking still affect whether an invitation is issued.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does living in Brampton increase my CRS score?

No. Express Entry is a federal system and a Brampton address does not add CRS points. Your score depends on the factors in IRCC’s CRS criteria and your accurately reported circumstances.

### Will any Brampton job offer qualify for the new points?

No. The announced Labour Market Integration factor applies only to qualifying circumstances under IRCC’s rules. Check the current occupation, wage, employer, and offer conditions; do not rely on a job title or an old LMIA alone.

### Should I change my profile before 3 November?

Correct inaccurate information whenever required and keep the supporting evidence. For the new factor, use IRCC’s live instructions and verify the displayed score after the transition rather than guessing how a field will be treated.
