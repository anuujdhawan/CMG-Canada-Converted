## Quick answer

Ontario’s 2026 Workforce Priority stream is an OINP route with distinct pathways, including job-offer pathways and an eligible self-employed physician pathway. Applicants must meet the requirements for a specific pathway and follow the current Expression of Interest process. Living in Brampton alone does not create eligibility or guarantee an invitation.

Ontario redesigned the OINP in 2026, closing its former streams and introducing the Ontario Workforce Priority stream. The provincial program nominates eligible applicants for permanent residence; IRCC makes the final decision on the federal permanent-residence application. The current details are on the [Ontario Workforce Priority stream page](https://www.ontario.ca/page/ontario-workforce-priority-stream) and [2026 OINP updates page](https://www.ontario.ca/page/2026-ontario-immigrant-nominee-program-updates).

The stream has job-offer pathways for workers in different TEER groups and a pathway for eligible self-employed physicians. Requirements differ by pathway. They may include employer participation, a full-time permanent Ontario job offer, specified work experience, minimum education, language results, and an invitation through the Expression of Interest system. Do not rely on an older OINP guide that describes one of the streams Ontario has now closed.

## What a Brampton applicant should do

Start by identifying the exact pathway and reading its current requirements. If the pathway requires a job offer, the employer must begin the process in Ontario’s Employer Portal before the worker registers an Expression of Interest. A job offer is not the same as an invitation or a nomination.

Keep the employer’s written offer, job description, wage and work-location details aligned with the information submitted to Ontario. Ask who is responsible for each portal step and save copies of confirmations. Check the stream page for the latest EOI opening, selection, and submission deadlines; missing an employer or applicant deadline can end the opportunity.

If you live in Brampton and work elsewhere in Ontario, use the actual employer and worksite facts in the application. Do not assume that living in the GTA makes a candidate eligible for any regional or employer pathway.

## Understand the sequence before you register

Read the pathway page from beginning to end and identify which applicant type it covers. For a job-offer route, check both the worker’s criteria and the employer’s criteria. The worker should confirm the occupation and duties, education, language, experience, and any other pathway-specific conditions. The employer should verify that the business and offered position satisfy Ontario’s current requirements.

The process has distinct stages: an employer may need to submit the job offer through Ontario’s Employer Portal; the worker then registers an Expression of Interest if eligible; Ontario may invite selected candidates; an invited applicant submits a complete nomination application by the stated deadline; and, if nominated, the candidate applies to the federal government for permanent residence. A job offer, an EOI registration, an invitation, a provincial nomination, and permanent residence are different steps.

Before an employer submits anything, reconcile the job title, duties, NOC/TEER, wage, hours, work location, and employment terms across the offer and supporting records. If a mismatch is real, clarify it before filing. Keep portal confirmations and assign someone to monitor official messages and deadlines.

Brampton is in Ontario, but local residence does not automatically make a role a regional pathway or satisfy a stream condition. Use the worksite and employer facts requested by Ontario. Because the program was redesigned in 2026, archive older checklists and verify every requirement on the live Ontario page immediately before registering or applying.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is the Ontario Workforce Priority stream the same as Express Entry?

No. OINP is a provincial nomination program with its own process. A nomination may support a later federal permanent-residence application, but Ontario’s EOI and nomination steps are separate from Express Entry selection.

### Can I register an EOI before my employer submits the offer?

For job-offer pathways, Ontario’s process places an employer step before the worker’s EOI. Confirm the exact current sequence and eligibility on the official stream page; do not rely on a submission receipt as an invitation.

### Does every Brampton job offer qualify?

No. The offer and employer must meet the selected pathway’s requirements, and the worker must also qualify. The city name or a permanent job offer by itself does not establish eligibility.
