## Quick answer

A PGWP is available only to graduates who meet IRCC’s eligibility rules; graduation alone does not guarantee one. Check the DLI and program, study history, applicable language requirement, field-of-study rules for your program and application date, and the 180-day application window. Verify the exact program and CIP code before relying on a school’s claim.

A post-graduation work permit is not automatic after graduation. Eligibility depends on the DLI and program, study history, application timing, language evidence, and, for some programs, the field-of-study requirement. IRCC’s general rule says an eligible graduate must apply within 180 days after receiving confirmation that the program is complete. Requirements differ between degree and non-degree programs, so check the [PGWP eligibility page](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/study-canada/work/after-graduation/eligibility.html) before assuming a program qualifies.

For 2026, IRCC says it will not add or remove fields from the PGWP-eligible list during the year. That does not mean every program is eligible: applicants who are subject to the field-of-study rule still need to check the exact six-digit CIP code and the date their study-permit application was submitted. See the live [field-of-study list](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/study-canada/work/after-graduation/eligibility/field-of-study.html).

## What Brampton graduates should check

Before the final term ends, save your transcript, completion letter, program details, language test results if required, and the school’s DLI information. Check whether you maintained the required full-time status and whether your program or application date puts you under a language or field-of-study requirement.

Do not choose a program solely on a promise that it leads to a work permit or permanent residence. A school’s marketing statement is not an IRCC decision. Ask for the DLI and program information in writing and compare it with IRCC’s current list.

## Check your PGWP file before the 180-day deadline

Create one folder before your school confirms completion. Save your final transcript, completion letter, program name and length, DLI information, study-permit history, language-test result if required, and any documents explaining a change in full-time status. The application deadline is tied to written confirmation that you completed the program, so record the date on the letter or transcript and do not wait until your permit is close to expiring.

Next, check the rules that apply to your specific program. The field-of-study requirement affects some non-degree programs and is tied to the program’s six-digit Classification of Instructional Programs (CIP) code and relevant study-permit application date. If the field requirement applies, compare the code exactly against IRCC’s eligible list. A program title that sounds similar is not proof of a matching code. IRCC has said it will not add or remove fields from the list during 2026, but that does not make every program eligible.

Also confirm the language requirement and the required level for your type of credential. Review whether your study history meets the full-time study requirements and whether any permitted exception applies. If you completed more than one program, do not assume that all program lengths or eligibility periods can be combined; read the rules for that combination.

If you are unsure, request written confirmation from the DLI of the program’s official name, credential, dates, and CIP code, then compare it with IRCC’s page. A PGWP is generally a one-time opportunity. Get the facts right before submitting, and keep a complete copy of the application.

## Frequently asked questions

### How long do I have to apply for a PGWP after graduation?

IRCC generally gives eligible graduates 180 days after receiving confirmation that they completed the program. Record the date on the completion evidence and check the current instructions for when the period starts.

### Does every college program in Brampton qualify for a PGWP?

No. Eligibility depends on the DLI, the program, your study history, and other requirements. Confirm the exact program with IRCC rather than relying only on school advertising.

### Is every field-of-study rule frozen for 2026?

IRCC says it will not add or remove fields from the eligible list during 2026. Applicants who are subject to the rule must still verify their specific program and CIP code against the live instructions.
