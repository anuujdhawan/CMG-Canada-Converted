## Quick answer

Inland and outland spouse sponsorship refer to different application classes and living situations. The right class depends on where the sponsored person lives, whether the couple is together in Canada, sponsor eligibility, and travel and status plans. Both routes require an eligible sponsor, a genuine qualifying relationship, complete evidence, and a separate federal assessment.

Canadian citizens and permanent residents may be able to sponsor an eligible spouse or partner for permanent residence. Where the applicant lives and whether they are living with the sponsor in Canada can affect which application class is available. The choice also affects travel and temporary-status planning, so “inland” and “outland” should not be treated as interchangeable labels.

An in-Canada class may be relevant when the sponsored spouse or partner lives with the sponsor in Canada and meets the applicable conditions. A family-class application can be used in other situations, including where the applicant is outside Canada. The sponsor and applicant must still meet the relevant eligibility and relationship requirements. Use the [official sponsorship guide](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/application/application-forms-guides/guide-5289-sponsor-your-spouse-common-law-partner-conjugal-partner-dependent-child-complete-guide.html) to identify the correct package.

When comparing inland versus outland spousal sponsorship in Brampton, start with where the applicant will live during processing and what travel or status constraints apply.

## Choose the class by facts, travel, and status

An in-Canada class is generally relevant when the spouse or partner lives with the sponsor in Canada and the application meets that class’s conditions. A family-class application may be appropriate when the sponsored person is outside Canada or when the facts fit that class. A Brampton postal address does not decide the class; the applicant’s actual residence and circumstances do.

For each option, consider where the sponsored person will live during processing, whether they have temporary status, whether they may need to travel, and how a departure from Canada could affect the plan. A person in Canada may be eligible to apply for an open work permit under the applicable sponsorship policy, but they must meet its conditions. Do not assume that submitting the sponsorship package by itself authorizes work or extends status.

The sponsor must meet the current requirements, including age and status, financial obligations from past sponsorships, and any applicable undertaking. A permanent-resident sponsor must live in Canada. A Canadian-citizen sponsor living abroad may sponsor if they can show they intend to live in Canada when the sponsored person becomes a permanent resident. The couple must also document that they meet the spouse, common-law, or conjugal-partner definition.

Before filing, use the current IRCC guide to select the correct package and forms. Check country-specific civil-document instructions, translations, police certificates, medical requirements, and whether previous marriages or sponsorships need to be documented. Processing estimates change, so consult IRCC’s tool close to filing and do not plan travel around a guaranteed date.

If the application is returned, refused, or affected by a status issue, identify the specific problem before choosing a new class or reapplying. The two routes are not simply faster and slower versions of the same file.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I sponsor my spouse if I live in Brampton and they are abroad?

A Canadian citizen or permanent resident may be able to sponsor an eligible spouse through the family class if the sponsor and applicant meet the requirements. The correct class depends on the full facts.

### Can a permanent resident sponsor a spouse while living outside Canada?

A permanent-resident sponsor must live in Canada. A Canadian citizen living abroad may be able to sponsor if they demonstrate an intention to return to Canada when the spouse becomes a permanent resident.

### Does an inland sponsorship application let my spouse work?

Not by itself. The sponsored person may qualify for a separate open work permit under current policy, but must meet those requirements and follow status and work-authorization rules.
