## Quick answer

If you apply to extend a work permit before it expires and remain in Canada, you may be able to stay under maintained status and keep working under the same conditions while IRCC decides. Restoration is different: after status expires, you generally must stop working and apply within the permitted period if eligible. Check your exact dates and permit conditions.

A worker who applies to extend a work permit before it expires may be able to remain in Canada under maintained status while IRCC decides, subject to the rules and conditions that apply. Restoration is different: it is for someone who has lost temporary resident status and may be eligible to apply to restore it. The person generally must stop working after losing status and may not resume until authorized.

IRCC says most restoration applicants must apply within 90 days of losing status, meet the conditions of their previous stay, and not have worked or studied without authorization. There is no guarantee that IRCC will approve restoration or a new permit. Read the official [restoration instructions](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/work-canada/restore.html) and the guidance for [extending a work permit](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/work-canada/extend.html).

## Make a date plan before the permit expires

Write down the expiry date printed on the permit, any separate “must leave Canada by” date, the date an extension was submitted, and the application confirmation. Keep a copy of the full application and proof of payment. If you are changing employers or permit conditions, check whether a new authorization is required before starting work.

For Brampton households, build in time to renew passports and gather employer documents. A short delay in requesting a letter or correcting an application can matter when a permit deadline is near.

Do not assume that leaving Canada and returning will preserve the same ability to work. Travel and re-entry can affect a person’s situation; review the official rules before booking travel. If status has already expired, stop work and get advice promptly rather than relying on a forum post or an old application receipt.


## Separate the two situations on your calendar

Situation A: you submitted a complete extension application before your permit expired. Save the submission confirmation and the full filed package. If you stay in Canada, maintained status may let you remain and continue working under the conditions of your expiring permit while IRCC processes the application. The exact work rights depend on what you applied for and your previous authorization. Do not change employers, occupation, or work location unless the rules allow it.

Situation B: your temporary resident status expired before you applied. You do not have maintained status from a late application. IRCC generally allows eligible people to apply to restore status within 90 days of losing it, but restoration is not guaranteed. Stop working after status is lost and do not resume until you receive the required authorization. A restoration application and a new work permit request may need to be submitted together under the current instructions.

Write down the expiry date on the permit, the date the extension was filed, the date status was lost if applicable, and any passport expiry or employer deadlines. If a visitor record or another permit type is involved, do not assume the work-permit rules apply in the same way. Use the relevant IRCC page for that status.

Travel can affect the ability to work under maintained status. Before leaving Canada, check IRCC’s current rules for returning and resuming work; do not rely on the fact that you have an application receipt. In Brampton, plan early for employer letters, passport renewal, and fees so a document delay does not turn a timely extension into a restoration problem.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I keep working after my work permit expires?

You may be able to work under maintained status if you applied to extend before expiry, remain in Canada, and meet the applicable conditions. If your status already expired, stop working until authorized.

### How long do I have to restore my status?

IRCC generally requires eligible applicants to apply within 90 days of losing status. Confirm the live requirements and your exact loss-of-status date before relying on that period.

### Can I travel while my work permit extension is processing?

Travel may affect your ability to work when you return. Check IRCC’s current travel and work instructions for your application type before leaving Canada.
