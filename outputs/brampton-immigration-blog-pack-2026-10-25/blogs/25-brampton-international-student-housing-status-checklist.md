## Before paying a deposit

Ask for a written lease or agreement, confirm who is renting the room, inspect the unit or arrange a trusted viewing, and get receipts for payments. Be cautious about pressure to send money before verifying the person and address. Keep a copy of the lease, landlord contact information, payment records, and messages.

## A safer move-in and records checklist

Before sending money, confirm the person has authority to rent the room, verify the property address, and arrange an in-person or trusted remote viewing. Get the rental terms in writing, including the room or unit, rent, deposit, included services, payment dates, and who lives in the home. Use traceable payment methods where possible and keep receipts, messages, and a copy of the agreement. Be cautious if someone pressures you to pay before viewing or refuses to provide basic identity and property details.

On move-in, keep a dated record of the room’s condition and any existing damage. Save the landlord or property manager’s contact details and emergency instructions. If the arrangement feels unsafe or the terms are unclear, seek tenant-support guidance from an appropriate Ontario service before signing or paying more.

Keep a separate immigration folder with your current study permit, DLI and program details, enrollment confirmation, school calendar, and copies of any IRCC application. A change of home address is not the same as changing a school, but update your contact details when required. If you plan to move to another institution, follow IRCC’s DLI-change procedure before making the transfer.

## Frequently asked questions

### What does Brampton’s 63.3% student housing statistic mean?

The City’s 2025 Housing Needs Assessment reports 63.3% of international students were living in unsuitable housing based on 2021 data. It is not a 2026 survey or a claim about every student.

### Does moving to another home in Brampton change my DLI?

No. A home address change is different from changing designated learning institutions. Keep contact information current and follow IRCC’s process if your school changes.

### What should I check before paying rent in Brampton?

Verify the landlord and address, view the unit or arrange a trusted viewing, get written rental terms, and keep receipts and communications. Seek local tenant support if the arrangement is unclear.
