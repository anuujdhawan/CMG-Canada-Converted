## How to use this pack

Every article here is a standalone guest post, written to be pitched to a third-party website — a blog, an industry publication, a directory or a news outlet — to earn a backlink to one specific page on commonwealthmigration.ca. Each article targets exactly one page, and that page is named in the article's backlink details block.

**What sits under each article heading**

- A **Backlink details** block, which is for your own outreach records. It gives the exact backlink URL, the two anchor texts the article already uses, the primary keyword and the category.
- The article itself, beginning at the first paragraph after that block.

**Please do not send the Backlink details block to a publisher.** It is internal metadata. Everything from the article's opening paragraph to the closing call to action is the publishable copy.

**How each article is built**

- **One article per page topic.** All 111 live topics are covered, in the site's own category order.
- **Two links per article.** One contextual link inside the body prose and one in the closing call to action — both to the same target page, both with descriptive anchor text. Nothing says "click here".
- **Unique angles throughout.** No two articles share an opening, structure or subheadings, so they can be placed on different sites without reading as duplicated content.
- **No invented figures.** The articles deliberately avoid quoting specific fees, CRS cut-offs, processing times or draw dates, and point readers to the official page instead — so they stay accurate as the rules change.

**If a publisher wants different anchor text,** keep the anchor descriptive and the URL unchanged. The suggested anchors are a starting point, not a requirement.

**Compliance note to keep intact when republishing.** Every article states that Commonwealth Migration Group is a private consultancy and not a government body, that no outcome is guaranteed, and that the information is general rather than legal advice. Please leave those sentences in.
