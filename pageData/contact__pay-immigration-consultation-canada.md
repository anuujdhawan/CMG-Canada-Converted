## Page Content

## Confirm your invoice before making a payment



The payment form is for clients who have already received an invoice or payment instruction. Review the amount, currency, service and payment timing before entering payment details. If you are unsure what the charge covers, ask for clarification before completing checkout.



- Check that you recognize the invoice
- Compare it with the service agreement
- Confirm any question with your consultant

## Check the service and payment terms



Your agreement should identify the service, who will provide it, the fee and what happens next. Read those terms before paying. If the work concerns a refusal, status expiry or response deadline, make sure the consultant knows the date and confirm which tasks are included in the agreed service.



- Confirm the scope and payment schedule in writing
- Ask which work is included in the fee
- Share urgent dates before work begins

## Protect your records and personal information



Keep a copy of your agreement, invoice and payment confirmation. Use the payment form only when its details match the instructions you received. Do not email card numbers or passwords. If a link, recipient or amount looks unfamiliar, verify it through the firm’s published contact channel before proceeding.



- Save the invoice and receipt
- Use the expected secure payment link
- Verify unusual requests before sending money

## Confirm government fees separately



Professional fees and government application fees are separate charges unless your agreement says otherwise. Check current government fees on the [IRCC fee list](https://ircc.canada.ca/english/information/fees/fees.asp) or the responsible province’s site before filing. Paying a representative does not pay an application fee unless the agreement explicitly includes that work.



- Check the fee with the responsible authority
- Read what the service agreement includes
- Keep proof of each payment

## Questions about paying for immigration services



Before retaining an immigration consultant, verify the person in the [CICC public register](https://register.college-ic.ca/Public-Register-EN/Public-Register-EN/Default.aspx) and make sure the agreement explains the service and fee. A consultant can provide advice and representation where authorized, but cannot control the decision made by IRCC, a province, the IRB or a court.



- Verify the representative's status
- Keep a signed copy of the agreement
- Understand what the payment covers

## Questions people ask



### How can I check the amount and service before paying an immigration invoice?

Compare the invoice amount, currency, service description and payment schedule with your signed agreement. If the recipient or payment link is different from what you expected, contact the office using its published details before completing the transaction.

### Does paying an immigration representative guarantee an application outcome?

No. Payment covers the professional service described in your agreement. IRCC, a province, the Immigration and Refugee Board or a court makes the decision under the rules and evidence that apply.

### What should I do if a payment page or invoice detail seems different?

Pause and contact the office through its published phone or email before submitting payment details. Do not rely on a new or unexpected link until the amount, recipient and service have been confirmed.

## A practical next step



If you have confirmed the invoice and agreement, continue through the secure payment form on this page. Keep the confirmation for your records. For questions about the service or amount, contact the office before paying.
